# Renewable Energy (Solar) Projects – PURE Curriculum

## Overview
This repository contains practical solar energy projects focusing on independent off-grid lighting system design and installation.

---

# Solar Powered Lighting System

## Project Title
Design and Installation of an Independent Solar-Powered Lighting System

## Objective
- Identify solar components
- Perform solar sizing calculations
- Wire a safe solar lighting system
- Understand DC vs AC configurations

---

## System Architecture Options

### Option 1 – DC Lighting (Efficient)
Solar Panel → Charge Controller → Battery → DC LED

### Option 2 – AC Lighting
Solar Panel → Charge Controller → Battery → Inverter → AC LED

---

## Required Components

### Solar Generation
- Solar panel (50W–200W)
- Mounting structure

### Storage
- 12V Deep-cycle battery
- Battery cables

### Regulation
- Charge controller (PWM/MPPT)
- DC fuse / breaker

### Conversion (Optional)
- 12V–220V inverter

### Tools
- Multimeter
- Wire stripper
- Screwdrivers

# Solar Sizing Example

Scenario:
- 2 bulbs
- 10W each
- 5 hours usage

Total Load:
20W

Daily Energy:
20W × 5h = 100Wh

Battery Size:
100Wh ÷ 12V = 8.3Ah  
Recommended: 12V 20Ah

Solar Panel Size:
100Wh ÷ 5h = 20W  
Recommended: 50W panel

---

# Installation Steps

1. Mount panel facing equator
2. Connect battery to controller FIRST
3. Connect solar panel
4. Connect load
5. Test voltage levels

---

# Testing Checklist

- Battery voltage: 12–13V
- Solar voltage: 18–22V
- Charging indicator active
- Lighting operational at night
